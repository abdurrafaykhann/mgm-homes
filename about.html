Why MGM

A co-host, not a takeover. You stay the owner in every sense.

Our approach

You didn’t buy a property to answer guest messages at midnight. We take the pricing, the replies and the scheduling, and leave you the decisions that are actually yours.

What We Hold To.

(1)

The listing stays yours.

Your accounts, your keys, your final say. We work inside your accounts as a co-host, and you can see or change anything at any time.

(2)

Judgement, not admin.

Anyone can send a check-in message. The value is in knowing when to hold a rate, when to drop it, and how to handle the awkward guest.

(3)

Your vendors stay yours.

We coordinate the cleaners and tradespeople you already trust. You keep contracting with them directly.

(4)

Everything in writing.

One note each week: occupancy, what we changed, and anything waiting on your decision. No surprises.
